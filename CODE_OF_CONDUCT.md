# Community conduct

Treat contributors/users respectfully. Discuss behavior and evidence; avoid personal
attacks, harassment, discrimination, doxxing and unwanted contact. Never post private
face data, addresses, credentials or communications without permission. Respect a
person's decision not to participate in face recognition.

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, close discussions or restrict
participation proportionately. Contact a maintainer privately through an available
GitHub channel for conduct concerns; no dedicated channel or response-time commitment
is established. Do not publish sensitive details to obtain attention. This applies
to repository issues, PRs and other project spaces managed by maintainers.
